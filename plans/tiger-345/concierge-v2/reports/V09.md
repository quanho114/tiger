## Changes

- `context.ts` builds canonical constraints/draft/pending state, bounded memory/history, untrusted-data policy and actual tool outputs.
- Hard input upper bounds: 6,000 per call, 12,000 per turn; at most 3 model calls and 6 tool executions. UTF-8 byte count plus framing is conservative, not a provider tokenizer measurement.
- `session-summary.ts` summarizes after 10 user turns or the byte budget and keeps four recent messages. Canonical allergy/draft state is never replaced by summary prose. Essentials that cannot fit fail safely.
