## Changes

- `customer-api/memory-handlers.ts` exposes authenticated inspect/settings/edit/delete/forget/restore with strict bodies and server-derived user identity.
- `MemoryControls.tsx` shows source and consent truth, edits food facts, forgets individual/all facts and explicitly restores suppressed behavioral learning. Identity-keyed mounting/abort signals prevent old account results from appearing after a switch.
- `20260922000004_concierge_memory_deletion_fence.sql` fences deletion/correction with epochs, retains behavioral suppression across epoch changes, and purges memory on account-deleting transition. The deletion worker purges before Auth deletion.
- Explicit extraction captures consent epoch before orchestration; delayed extraction cannot adopt a later disable/re-enable consent epoch.
