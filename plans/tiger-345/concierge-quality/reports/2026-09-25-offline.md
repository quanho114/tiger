## Implemented authority gates

- Catalog-dependent turns keep server-built cards and messages; model prose cannot replace an empty catalog result or invent a different price. Explicit dish queries filter candidates by the requested name/category/tags so unrelated menu items cannot stand in for missing dishes.
- Opening-hour questions query operational `business_closures` and `business_hours`, including tomorrow's closure. Storage errors and missing schedules fail closed; generic location answers omit stale hours in the published FAQ. Delivery fees without an address/zone redirect to the cart's quote instead of supplying a number.
- Short-form budgets (`800k`, `1tr5`, revisions), children, unaccented FAQ searches and topic ranking are deterministic. Published/effective filtering and the existing draft/allergy guards remain intact.

## Remaining acceptance work

- This fixture measures routing, a small set of state slots, document recall and specific factual regressions; it does **not** measure p50/p95 end-to-end latency, per-tool accuracy across all intents, full answer quality, cost, clarification burden or real-provider behavior. No metrics are invented for those fields.
- The current public response contract includes catalog provenance on menu cards but not a general typed fact record for hours and other operational assertions. That contract and a larger independently reviewed holdout need design/review before claiming Q1/Q2 complete for production.
- Planner optimization and persistent-memory changes were not warranted by these failures. Provider/staging acceptance, human review and rollout/rollback ownership remain gated by explicit approval under Q6; an offline pass does not authorize production enablement.

Rollback: revert only the routing, budget, RAG and orchestrator quality changes after a failing regression is reproduced; preserve the existing PostgreSQL session and draft contracts.
